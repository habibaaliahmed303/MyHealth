Machine Name

Smith Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Varies by exercise (Quadriceps, Chest, Shoulders)
  • Secondary: Glutes, Hamstrings, Triceps, Core

How to Use

  1. Position your body under or in front of the bar depending on the exercise.
  2. Set the bar height and load the desired weight.
  3. Grip the bar evenly on both sides.
  4. Unrack the bar by rotating it to release the hooks.
  5. Perform the movement along the fixed path of the machine.
  6. Maintain control throughout the full range of motion.
  7. Re-rack the bar by rotating it back into the hooks.

Proper Form

  • Keep a neutral spine throughout the movement.
  • Engage your core for stability.
  • Adjust your stance to match the bar path.
  • Control both lifting and lowering phases.

Common Mistakes

  • Poor body positioning relative to the bar path.
  • Relying too much on the machine for balance.
  • Locking joints aggressively.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Ignoring full range of motion.
💡 Quick Tip: Adjust your stance, not the bar path, to fit your natural movement.

Machine Name

Leg Extension Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Quadriceps
  • Secondary: None

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine with your back fully supported.
  2. Place your ankles behind the padded bar.
  3. Align your knees with the machine's pivot point.
  4. Grip the handles for stability.
  5. Extend your legs upward until almost straight.
  6. Squeeze your quadriceps at the top.
  7. Lower the weight slowly back to the start.

Proper Form

  • Keep your back flat against the seat.
  • Align knees with the machine axis.
  • Move in a slow, controlled range.
  • Keep toes pointing forward.

Common Mistakes

  • Swinging the weight instead of controlling it.
  • Locking the knees forcefully.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Lifting hips off the seat.
  • Partial range of motion.
💡 Quick Tip: Pause briefly at the top for a stronger quadriceps contraction.

Machine Name

Hack Squat Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Quadriceps
  • Secondary: Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves

How to Use

  1. Step into the machine and position your shoulders under the pads.
  2. Place your feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  3. Keep your back flat against the support.
  4. Release the safety handles.
  5. Lower your body by bending your knees slowly.
  6. Go down until your thighs are about parallel.
  7. Push through your heels to return up.
  8. Stop just before locking your knees.

Proper Form

  • Keep your back fully pressed against the pad.
  • Keep your knees aligned with your toes.
  • Engage your core throughout the movement.
  • Maintain a controlled tempo.

Common Mistakes

  • Letting the knees collapse inward.
  • Locking the knees at the top.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Lifting heels off the platform.
  • Not going deep enough.
💡 Quick Tip: Push through your heels, not your toes.

Machine Name

Seated Leg Curl Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Hamstrings
  • Secondary: Gastrocnemius

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine with your back fully supported.
  2. Align your knees with the machine's pivot point.
  3. Place your lower legs behind the padded lever.
  4. Secure your thighs under the support pads.
  5. Curl your legs back by bending your knees.
  6. Squeeze your hamstrings at the end of the movement.
  7. Return slowly to the starting position.

Proper Form

  • Keep your back firmly against the seat.
  • Do not lift your hips during the movement.
  • Control both curl and return phases.
  • Keep movement smooth and steady.

Common Mistakes

  • Using momentum instead of control.
  • Lifting hips off the seat.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Not using full range of motion.
  • Dropping the weight too quickly.
💡 Quick Tip: Hold the contraction for a second before returning slowly.

Machine Name

Hip Adductor Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Adductors (Inner Thigh)
  • Secondary: Gracilis, Pectineus

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine with your back supported.
  2. Place your legs outside the pads.
  3. Adjust the starting position for a comfortable stretch.
  4. Grip the handles for stability.
  5. Bring your legs inward against the pads.
  6. Squeeze your inner thighs at the end of the movement.
  7. Return slowly to the starting position.

Proper Form

  • Keep your back flat against the seat.
  • Move in a slow and controlled manner.
  • Avoid leaning forward or using momentum.
  • Keep your core engaged for stability.

Common Mistakes

  • Using momentum instead of muscle control.
  • Closing legs too fast without control.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Partial range of motion.
  • Lifting hips off the seat.
💡 Quick Tip: Focus on a slow squeeze inward and control the return phase.

Machine Name

Leg Press Machine

Target Muscles

  • Secondary: Glutes, Hamstrings, Calves

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine with your back flat against the pad.
  2. Place your feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  3. Release the safety handles.
  4. Lower the platform by bending your knees slowly.
  5. Stop when your knees reach about a 90-degree angle.
  6. Push through your heels to extend your legs.
  7. Stop just before locking your knees.

Proper Form

Common Mistakes

  • Lowering the weight too fast.
  • Letting the lower back lift off the seat.
  • Placing feet too low on the platform.
  • Using excessive weight.
💡 Quick Tip: Control the descent more than the ascent.

Machine Name

Seated Calf Raise Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Soleus
  • Secondary: Gastrocnemius

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine and place pads over your knees.
  2. Position the balls of your feet on the footrest.
  3. Adjust pad height to fit snugly.
  4. Push up onto your toes as high as possible.
  5. Hold briefly at the peak contraction.
  6. Lower your heels slowly below the footrest level.
  7. Repeat with full range of motion.

Proper Form

  • Keep your torso upright throughout.
  • Full stretch at the bottom adds muscle length.
  • Avoid bouncing at the bottom.
  • Perform slow, deliberate reps.

Common Mistakes

  • Using momentum to bounce the weight.
  • Not reaching full extension.
  • Placing feet incorrectly on the plate.
  • Using excessive weight.
  • Skipping the stretch at the bottom.
💡 Quick Tip: Go slow on the way down for maximum soleus activation.

Machine Name

Hip Abductor Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Gluteus Medius, Gluteus Minimus
  • Secondary: Tensor Fasciae Latae

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine with your back against the support.
  2. Place your legs inside the pads.
  3. Adjust the pads so your knees are at a comfortable position.
  4. Push your legs outward against the pads.
  5. Squeeze your outer glutes at the widest point.
  6. Return slowly to the starting position.
  7. Keep movements controlled throughout.

Proper Form

  • Sit tall and keep your core engaged.
  • Avoid leaning to one side.
  • Move symmetrically on both sides.
  • Slow, controlled reps beat fast ones.

Common Mistakes

  • Using too much weight and losing form.
  • Leaning the torso sideways.
  • Not achieving full range.
  • Bouncing or jerking the weight.
  • Lifting hips off the seat.
💡 Quick Tip: Squeeze and hold at the widest point for 1 second.

Machine Name

Standing Calf Raise Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Gastrocnemius
  • Secondary: Soleus

How to Use

  1. Step under the shoulder pads and position the balls of your feet on the platform.
  2. Keep your legs straight with a slight bend at the knee.
  3. Rise up onto your toes as high as possible.
  4. Hold at the top for a moment.
  5. Lower your heels as far as comfortable below the platform.
  6. Repeat with a full range of motion.

Proper Form

  • Keep your back straight and core tight.
  • Equal weight on both feet.
  • Rise fully onto the toes.
  • Control the descent fully.

Common Mistakes

  • Bending the knees excessively.
  • Using partial range of motion.
  • Rushing through reps.
  • Not loading the stretch at the bottom.
  • Using excessive weight.
💡 Quick Tip: Rise all the way up and lower all the way down for full engagement.

Machine Name

Glute Kickback Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Gluteus Maximus
  • Secondary: Hamstrings, Core

How to Use

  1. Position yourself on the machine per the manufacturer instructions.
  2. Secure your foot or lower leg against the pad.
  3. Keep your supporting leg stable.
  4. Push the pad back and up in a controlled arc.
  5. Squeeze your glute at the peak of the movement.
  6. Return slowly to the start without dropping the weight.
  7. Complete all reps on one side before switching.

Proper Form

  • Keep your hips square and level.
  • Avoid rotating your torso.
  • Engage your core throughout.
  • Focus on the glute, not the lower back.

Common Mistakes

  • Arching the lower back excessively.
  • Using momentum instead of glute contraction.
  • Not squeezing at the top.
  • Kicking too high and losing form.
  • Using excessive weight.
💡 Quick Tip: Think about pushing through the heel to better activate the glute.

Machine Name

Hip Thrust Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Gluteus Maximus
  • Secondary: Hamstrings, Core, Hip Flexors

How to Use

  1. Sit on the machine and position the pad across your hips.
  2. Plant your feet flat on the platform shoulder-width apart.
  3. Lean back against the support.
  4. Push your hips upward by squeezing your glutes.
  5. Drive up until your body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders.
  6. Hold at the top for a beat.
  7. Lower your hips slowly back down.

Proper Form

  • Keep your chin tucked and core braced.
  • Feet flat and knees tracking over toes.
  • Drive through heels, not toes.
  • Full extension at the top.

Common Mistakes

  • Hyperextending the lower back at the top.
  • Not driving through the heels.
  • Using momentum to swing the weight.
  • Incomplete range of motion.
  • Excessive weight too early.
💡 Quick Tip: Squeeze your glutes hard at the top and hold for 2 seconds.

Machine Name

Lying Leg Curl Machine

Target Muscles

  • Primary: Hamstrings
  • Secondary: Gastrocnemius, Glutes

How to Use

  1. Lie face-down on the machine pad.
  2. Align your knees just over the edge of the bench.
  3. Place your ankles under the padded lever.
  4. Hold the handles for support.
  5. Curl your legs toward your glutes by bending the knees.
  6. Squeeze your hamstrings at full flexion.
  7. Lower the weight slowly and with control.

Proper Form

  • Keep your hips pressed down throughout.
  • Avoid arching your lower back.
  • Use smooth, controlled movement.
  • Flex at the knee, not the hip.

Common Mistakes

  • Lifting your hips to assist the curl.
  • Using too much weight.
  • Dropping the weight without control.
  • Not achieving full range of motion.
  • Jerking through the movement.
💡 Quick Tip: Point your toes during the curl for a stronger hamstring contraction.
