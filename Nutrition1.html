# Nutrition

Food is a source of energy,yet it is a double-edged sword:overeating can lead to obesity while undereating can lead to being underweight

# Macronutrient Distribution

The amount a person needs daily of carbohydrates, protein, and healthy fats.

*Protein

-Percentage: 10% to 35% of total calorie Daily requirement:

-Average person: 0.8 to 1g per kg

-Athlete: 1.6 to 2.2g per kg

-1g protein = 4 calories

*Carbohydrates

-Percentage: 45% to 65% of total calories

-Best sources:

  • Oats

  • Brown rice

  • Potatoes

-1g carbs = 4 calories

* Healthy Fats

-Percentage: 20% to 35% of total calories

-Best sources:

  • Olive oil

  • Nuts

  • Avocado

-1g fat = 9 calories

*Daily Water Intake

-Drink 8 cups daily (about 2 liters)

-More accurate calculation:

-Water intake = weight (kg) × 0.033

-Example: A person weighing 70 kg: 70 × 0.033 = 2.3 liters per day

-Important Notes:

Water needs increase in:-

  • Hot weather

  • During exercise

  • Excessive sweating

# Daily Calorie Calculation (Simple & Organized Guide)

Your daily calorie needs are calculated in two main steps:

* First: Calculate Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

-This is the number of calories your body needs to maintain basic vital functions
(like breathing and blood circulation) without any activity.

*Formulas

*For Men:

(10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) + 5

*For Women:

(10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) − (5 × age) − 161

Go to the website that calculates BMR https://tdeecalculator.net

*Second: Calculate Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)

- After calculating your BMR, multiply the result by your activity level:

- Activity Level Multiplier:-

  • Sedentary (little or no activity) BMR × 1.2

  • Light activity (1–3 days/week) BMR × 1.375

  • Moderate activity (3–5 days/week) BMR × 1.55

  • High activity (6–7 days/week) BMR × 1.725

Go to the website that calculates TDEE https://tdeecalculator.net

#What are healthy energy drinks?

Healthy energy drinks are natural or low-sugar drinks that help increase energy, focus, and alertness without harming the body.

  1. Increased energy and focus

  2. Improved mental performance

  3. Better physical performance

  4. Rich in antioxidants

  5. Better hydration than processed drinks

< Here are some tips>

  • Drink enough water every day to stay hydrated and support your body functions

  • Eat a balanced diet that includes protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats

  • Avoid overeating and try to control your portion sizes

  • Eat more fruits and vegetables for vitamins and minerals

  • Reduce sugar and processed food intake

  • Listen to your body and eat when you are hungry, not bored
