🌙 Sleep: The Hidden Engine of Performance

Why Sleep is Important

Sleep is essential for both mental and physical health. It allows your body to recover, your brain to reset, and your energy to be restored.

1. Benefits for Athletes

Muscle Recovery: Growth hormone is released during deep sleep, helping muscles grow and repair.

Fat Burning: Good sleep balances hunger hormones and reduces cravings.

Better Focus: Improves reaction time, coordination, and reduces injuries.

2. The Success Formula

Training + Nutrition + Sleep = Success

If sleep = 0 → Results ≈ 0

3. Healthy Sleep Tips

✔ Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day

✔ Avoid screens before sleep

✔ Keep your room dark and cool

✔ Do relaxing activities before bed

4. Golden Rules

Fixed Wake-Up Time: Keep it consistent.

Sunlight: Get morning light daily.

20-Min Rule: If you can't sleep, get up and relax.

No All-Nighters: Sleep early, wake early.
